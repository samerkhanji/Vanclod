Full For features and interviews

Vanclod — also known as Vanclod Jandam — is a Beirut-based DJ and producer. Active in nightlife since 2002 and behind the decks since 2008, he has spent more than two decades contributing to the growth of Lebanon's electronic music scene, both on stage and behind it.

Known for his blend of Indie Dance and Tech House, Vanclod has built a career that reaches far beyond DJing. Alongside performing, he has helped develop and run some of Lebanon's most influential clubs, events and festivals — platforms that have welcomed many of the world's leading electronic artists. As a co-founder, he helped build The Grand Factory, Reunion, AHM and 2ND SUN. He is also one of the founders of C U NXT SAT, one of Beirut's most recognised parties.

Over the years he has shared the stage with Adriatique, Tale Of Us, Stephan Bodzin, Mathame, ARTBAT, Boris Brejcha, Kevin de Vries and Solomun, among many others. His sets are thoughtful and adaptable, reflecting years of experience reading a room and shaping a night.

Alongside a busy touring schedule, Vanclod is focused on the next chapter of his career as a producer. His studio work is a natural extension of everything he's built — drawing on decades immersed in electronic music while developing a sound that reflects his own identity rather than chasing trends.
